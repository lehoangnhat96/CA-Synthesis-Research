## **2. Experimental** 

_2.1. Materials_ 

_2.2. Preparation_ 

Graphene oxide (GO) sheets were synthesized based on the well-known Hummers’ method but with some modification [25]. The 3D-NGA hydrogel was prepared using the following procedure: typically, 15 mg DA was added into a GO aqueous dispersion (15 mL, 1 mg mL[[-1]] ). The dispersion pH was adjusted to about 8.0, and then sonicated for 40 min. The mixture was sealed in a 20 mL Teflon-lined autoclave and maintained at 180[[o]] C for 12 h. After cooled to room temperature, the as-prepared hydrogel was washed with ethanol and water and then freeze-dried. Ultralight NGA was obtained after heating the freeze-dried graphene gel at 800[o] C for 3 h under an Ar atmosphere. Simultaneously, 3D GA was synthesized by the same method in the absence of DA. To construct an electrode, the freestanding of cylindrical 3D NGA was cut into piece (1 cm × 0.5 cm, 0.3 mm 

thick). Then the piece was fixed onto a glass slide, and an electrical lead was made by copper paste and copper wire insulated with silicone rubber. 

The graphite powder was purchased from Lvyin Co. (China). 5% Nafion ethanol solution and dopamine (DA) were from Aldrich Chem. Co. (USA). All other reagents were purchased from from Sinopharm Chemical Reagent Ltd. Co. (China), and were of analytical grade and used without further purification. The pure water from a Millipore Autopure WR600A system (USA) was used for the solution preparation.
